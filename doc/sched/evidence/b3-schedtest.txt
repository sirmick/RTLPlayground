## Stack paint and deep (added after review, 2026-11-18)

rtlsim, boot then 1.5 s idle; then deep 9 + a 150-char paste + print 200; then deep 0:

    idle: paint_from 8a hw 9f step SP 8a
    deep 9 + paste + print: hw c2 deep SP a8 runs 57, RX overruns 36
    idata 0xa0-0xc0: 0900000600024ef90379f9c23203c00e000102b9c0c16c35c1656502007c0241
    checks: []

Peak stack: 0x9f - 0x7c = 35 B idle/commands; 0xc2 - 0x7c = 70 B with the 9-deep chain under the console ISRs
(61 B to 0xff). The RX overruns are the deep step's 5-10 ms spin with input flowing: a 64 B ring fills
in 5.5 ms at 115200, as console.md's ~5 ms rule for con says.
