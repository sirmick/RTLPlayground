## Found on the way

- The Makefile reads DEPS before schedtest.mk adds its sources: after deep's extra slot, sched_bank.rel
  kept the old T_FIXED and slot 12 (slow) started free (jobs claimed 12 slots). Fixed in 2d69a5e.
- hot's yield kept every pass busy, so the simulator could not skip idle time: 10 s of firmware took 6.5 s
  busy, 0.9 s idle. hot is now on only for its test; the soak runs everything else.
