The design (core.md "Measurement")
----------------------------------
- sched_st_runs[] counts this second's runs, 16 bits, no saturation test.
- The roll is the dispatcher's, at sched_m_pass_end(): at the first pass
  end at or past sched_roll_tick, the counts go into sched_st_rate[] and
  are zeroed, the time asleep becomes sched_load. The grid is fixed (200
  ticks), half a second off house's and loop's: a late roll (by at most
  the pass that crossed) moves time to the next second, not out of the
  average; a pass over a second restarts the grid. With the half-second
  offset a 1/s state can't flip between 0 and 2. Chosen over HOUSE_SEC: no task's
  lateness in it, and nothing for a task to forget.
- Time asleep: sched_idle() reads the fine clock before its check and
  after the wake; each sleep ends at the next interrupt (the tick's at the
  latest), so it's at most a tick plus the waking ISR, and fine_elapsed()
  counts a tick inside it. Timer 2 runs in IDL (its ISR wakes the CPU;
  rtlsim advances it while idle-skipping). Summed in 1% steps of a
  second (2 ticks, sl_unit = 1626 units) plus a 16-bit remainder that
  carries: 3 B, not the 4 B two-halves value of the brief, the same
  arithmetic rule (memory.md rule 3), no division.
- sched_roll_tick is written last in roll(): a tool that stops the
  firmware anywhere and sees it move sees the whole second (3e3df73,
  found by flash_job_steps S5: a sample mid-roll lost 5 erases).

Findings, open
--------------
1. The idle floor is ~22 %, not ~0 (node body: "load near 0% idle"). It is
   real: rtlsim's own busy cycles agree to 0.1 point. 401 passes/s, two a
   tick (the one that runs the tick tasks, then an empty one before the
   sleep), each over the 72 run_seq positions, plus NET/STP/LACP/LLDP_TICK
   every tick: ~1.1 ms of each 5 ms tick. O2's "idle positions cost a load
   and a compare" is about 22 % of the CPU at idle; a smaller run sequence
   or skipping the empty pass would lower it. Not changed (not FR's).
   Silicon may differ from the sim's cycle model: on the 8x after F3.
2. The first second after boot is half a second (rates and load read half).
3. Load is -0/+1 point (1% steps); the waking ISR counts as asleep.
4. ovs-rtlpg's long_steps needed the header change: done by the orchestrator
   (dff814d). Before it, the check would have been skipped silently.
