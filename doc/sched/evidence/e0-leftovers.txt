## 1. The crash record carries the image's identity

DB (silicon.md): the first boot of db07bb9 printed a record schedtest had
left at 0xbfe0 (a web update keeps xdata), decoded with the firmware's slot
and state names.
- sched.mk writes $(BUILDDIR)/sched/build_id.h each build (FORCE + cmp, as
  version.h): CRASH_IMAGE_HI/_LO, 32 bits of a SHA-1 over the version
  (v0.1.0-<hash>[-dirty]), the build date and the build directory (machine,
  layout, schedtest and its ST_NEG variants). So another commit, another
  board, schedtest, or another build of the same commit is another image.
- crash.h: bytes 24-27 of the record (from the spare 7) hold it; the record
  stays 32 B and the sum still covers bytes 0-29. crash_die() stores it.
  crash_boot() takes a record only if magic, sum and identity hold;
  another image's valid record is dropped (magic cleared), unprinted, and
  crash_prior stays 0. watchdog.md has the layout.
- Only crash.c and sched_health.c include build_id.h, so only they are
  rebuilt for a new identity (they were already rebuilt by version.h's date).
- Host (test_sched): the record carries the identity; a valid record with
  another identity is ignored, nothing printed or kept, and dropped.
- Sim (tools/crash_foreign.py FW MAP SCHEDTEST): schedtest is reset with
  ^R ^R; its record is put in the firmware's xdata and the firmware is
  chip-reset (xdata kept, as on silicon):
| firmware | C1: schedtest's record | C2: the firmware's own (^R ^R) |
|---|---|---|
| f621897 (before E0) | FAIL: "Last reset: ^R ^R in rx/RX_IDLE after 0 ms, PC 0x3161 bank 1, states LOOP_POLL ? LOOP_POLL ..." (DB's misreport) | PASS |
| 56ba466 | PASS: no "Last reset:", crash_last empty | PASS: "Last reset: ^R ^R in con/CON_EDIT after 0 ms, PC 0xf101 bank 2, ..." |
  The schedtest image is from the same commit (56ba466), so the identity
  tells apart two images built from the same source.

## 3. HOUSE_SEC ~41 ms on silicon: loader_confirm()'s sector erase

Reproduced in rtlsim (8x, 56ba466; the control mode times the flash as the
chip does, a sector erase ~45 ms):
    at 15.1 s, before loader_confirm: HOUSE_SEC 4 runs, max 36us; overruns 0
    loader_confirm at 17.114 s; flash_sector_erase at 17.114422 s
    at 19.1 s, after: HOUSE_SEC 8 runs, max 40000us; overruns 2 last HOUSE_SEC
(/tmp/c/v4/house3.py: health tasks before and after, run_until_pc on
_loader_confirm and _flash_sector_erase.) Silicon's 40.8 ms is the same.
- The call: house_second() -> loader_confirm() (boot.c) once,
  LDR_CONFIRM_S (15 s) after boot: the loader writes its boot-count mark
  "BC" at LDR_BOOTCNT (0x3a000) on every boot through it, and the app
  confirms a good boot by erasing that sector, a ~45 ms
  flash_sector_erase() that waits in the common area. So every boot
  through the loader has one ~40 ms HOUSE_SEC step, ~15 s in.
- Not cheap to fix here: an erase can't be shortened, and banked code may
  not run while the flash is busy (flash.md's rule), so the wait can't be
  spread over steps without the flash-busy mode flash.md declines. The book
  allows an erase as the one exception to the 20 ms limit, but as the flash
  job's FL_ERASE step.
- Owner: E3 (the flash job, sched5): loader_confirm becomes a flash-job
  request (erase LDR_BOOTCNT, no program), so the 40 ms is an FL_ERASE step
  and HOUSE_SEC stays short. Until then sched_tour's long-step check and
  EH's long-step checks will see HOUSE_SEC 40 ms once a boot.
- The earlier sim tours didn't show it (HOUSE_SEC 49 us) because the tour
  clears the stats (`health clear`) before each command, after the erase.
