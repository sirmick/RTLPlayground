## What E3 does

1. The flash job (flash_job.c, tasks.def `flash`: FL_ERASE, FL_PROG,
   FL_VERIFY, FL_DONE; flash.md "As built"). Each step's erase or program
   waits in the common area (rtl837x_flash.c, unchanged) before it returns:
   no banked code runs while the flash is busy. Requests: FL_UPLOAD,
   FL_CONFIG, FL_BOOTCNT, FL_FACTORY(_BTN), through flash_job_start().
2. Upload: httpd's stream_upload() split at its flash writes into
   upload_prog() (FL_PROG: parse the stage, program up to 2 pages a step,
   FL_ERASE before each sector's first page), upload_verify() (FL_VERIFY:
   the read-back CRC 2 KiB a step, then the answer) and upload_done()
   (FL_DONE: more came meanwhile -> FL_PROG again, else the stage starts
   again). The answer and window updates go out on net's poll after
   EV_UPLOAD_DONE (uip_poll_conn), at once.
3. Flow control by the receive window (decision 20, net.md item 1): the
   stage is config_buf (0 B: uIP has one connection, the #error at
   webcmd_queue()); UIP_CONF_RECEIVE_WINDOW = uip_rcv_wnd = the stage's
   room, at most an MSS. No uip_stop()/uip_restart() anywhere. While the job
   writes for a connection that has ended, a new connection is reset.
4. Config save (POST /config): the body is buffered whole as before, then
   FL_ERASE + FL_PROG (512 B a step); data meanwhile dropped; the 200 on the
   poll after the job. Same job, same one-connection argument.
5. Factory reset (console `factory-reset`, the button): FL_ERASE + 8 x
   FL_PROG from the default sector, then FL_DONE prints and resets; cli
   shows no prompt meanwhile. Busy: "Flash busy, not reset".
6. loader_confirm() (orchestrator, from E0): its boot-count erase is one
   FL_ERASE (FL_BOOTCNT); house asks again next second if the job is busy.
   HOUSE_SEC 40 ms -> 0.1 ms.
7. CRASH_UPDATE (orchestrator, from E0): dropped, 4 reserved; watchdog.md
   says why (the record would be read by another image; the loader's lines
   report an update).

## O3: uip_stop() against Linux (QA e3-o3-window)

tools/curl_upload.py (rtlswitch Lab: curl -F from a netns on front port 1,
tcpdump, rtlsim paced to the wall clock). At 19fe58c (the book's design,
uip_stop() after each segment): config save fine; firmware uploads stall.
uIP advertises 1460 B; Linux sends 2 x 730 B per window (half the largest
window seen); after the first we stop, the ACK says 0, the second, in
flight, is dropped without an ACK and comes back only on RTO, with backoff
(0.2 ... 26 s): 0.5 KiB/s, uIP resets the connection at 82 s after 9
segments. Approved: the window stage (3846853).

poll's update said 0 (the room hadn't changed), and uIP doesn't answer
Linux's zero-window probes: 30 s silences, then a reset. f7100d1: the stage
starts again only in FL_DONE, which goes back to FL_PROG if more came.

## Host tests (make -C test quick: QUICK TESTS PASSED, test_loader skipped)

test_httpd_tx (both layouts) drives httpd.c and uip.c unmodified; its flash
job stand-in calls the same upload_prog/upload_verify/upload_done, at once
or a step at a time (job_defer). E3's scenarios:
- upload window: segments as Linux sends them (half an MSS) with the job
  held, then run a step at a time between arrivals: every segment inside
  the window taken; the window closes as the stage fills and the poll after
  the job reopens it; no ACK moves the right edge (ack + window) left,
  checked on every ACK the switch sends; a 5000 B image staged whole across
  two sectors, each erased once before its first page; the answer after;
  a segment past the window is refused (400).
  Mutation check: closing a short window to 0 (uip_stop's effect) fails
  "no ACK moves the window's right edge left".
- upload window race: segments between the job's last write and its
  FL_DONE fill the window; they are written and the window reopens.
- flash busy: an upload refused at once when the job is busy; a new
  connection reset while it runs.
- the existing upload/config scenarios, through the stand-in (the poll sends
  the config's 200).

## Review fix: no other flash job between an upload's segments (7e30e5e)

Reviewer: between two segments the upload's job has ended, so another kind
(FL_BOOTCNT from loader_confirm() ~15 s after a boot, a console
factory-reset) could start; a segment staged meanwhile was left to that
job (upload_go()), whose FL_DONE ended without it: unwritten, and if last,
no answer and no timeout (a factory reset would also overwrite flash_buf).
Fix: (a) up_active, set from the first staged segment to the end of the
part or of the connection; flash_job_start() refuses every other kind
meanwhile (loader_confirm() asks again next second; factory-reset and the
button: "Flash busy, not reset"); an upload whose body stops coming is
aborted after 30 s (UPLOAD_TIMEOUT, the postbody timer) so it can't hold
them off for ever. (b) FL_DONE of any kind but a factory reset goes on with
the upload (FL_PROG) if the stage holds unwritten bytes.
Code: xdata +1 B (18031), code +87 B (8x 160987); gate PASS as below
(data 98, stack 138, common 0x3d7d; banks 902/462/846 free, SWGT024
883/833/453).
- Host: scenario_upload_during_other_job: with an upload between segments,
  FL_BOOTCNT doesn't start; the last segment staged while an FL_BOOTCNT job
  runs is written and answered. It FAILs with the old FL_DONE rule (the
  stand-in's fl_end() going on only for FL_UPLOAD), PASSes with the fix.
- Sim: tools/flash_job_steps.py S5: a refused upload started at 13.0 s
  after power-on, over the loader's confirm (~17.6 s in the sim): answer
  400, the staged image = the payload, the boot count erased after it
  (FL_ERASE 58 at the answer, 59 three seconds later). PASS on both boards.
  It passes on f7100d1 too: house found the upload's job running each
  second, and the gaps between segments are too narrow to hit; the host
  test is the one that shows the race.
