## What changed

- sfp0/sfp1 (tasks.c, slot order unchanged): SFP_START, SFP_POLL (pins every
  50 ms, the slot's arg its poll tick), SFP_WAKE (polling on through the
  wake-up delay), SFP_READ (one transaction a step: rate+encoding, the ASCII
  fields x3, options, vendor, model, serial, then the quirks' at most one;
  the old output, in order), SFP_CONFIG (sds_config), SFP_DDM (4
  transactions, 0x51 184..239, every 40 polls = 2 s).
- sfp.c: sfp_pins() (the old pin logic; 1 when the wake-up delay is due,
  where the old poll read the module), sfp_read_next(), sfp_configure(),
  sfp_ddm_wanted(), sfp_ddm_next(): one slot each, one transaction, a
  status; the steps choose the state (decision 22, the sweep's check).
- httpd/page_impl.c: send_sfp_info() and sfp_send_data() read sfp_id and
  sfp_diag. A cache not yet filled gives what a failed read gave (an empty
  field). /information.json's module text is there once SFP_READ has read
  it (~0.5 s after insertion), not at once.
- rtl837x_pins.c sfp_read_block(): gives up when the controller is still
  busy after 1-2 ticks (5-10 ms) instead of spinning. Not reachable in the
  sim (its I2C answers at once).
- cli `sfp N <speed>`: sfp_pins(slot) at once (was handle_sfp(), both
  slots); `sfp` still reads the module live (a diagnostic).

  statics). schedtest (8x): 380/746/239 free.

    sfp reg 0418/0420/0424: the I2C controller's last transaction is the

      cage (a failed read), the new page doesn't touch I2C.

  console are equal in the sfp scenario.
