## Tasks (tasks.def) and what each runs

| slot | task | freq | state(s) | runs | wakes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | rx | high | RX_IDLE, RX_STEP | rx_service(): handle_rx (RX_BUDGET 4), RXIS, re-arm EX1 | EV_RX; yields while frames wait |
| 1 | link | high | LINK_READ | link_service(): read/clear LINK_CHG, EX0, check_links, lacp_link_change | EV_LINK (INT0, and house's link_irq once a second, as before) |
| 2 | loop | high | LOOP_START, LOOP_POLL | rldp_tick() if rldp_enabled | 1 s, task_until(last + 200) |
| 3 | con | high | CON_EDIT | cmd_edit() + the pending command (today's code, until D3) | EV_CON_RX |
| 4 | net | once | NET_TICK | handle_tx() (uIP periodic) | every tick |
| 5 | stp | medium | STP_TICK | the 50 Hz stp_timers() catch-up (<= 8) if stp_enabled | every tick |
| 6 | lacp | medium | LACP_TICK | lacp_timers() if lacpEnabled | every tick |
| 7 | lldp | once | LLDP_TICK | lldp_tick(), cabletest_tick() | every tick |
| 8, 9 | sfp0, sfp1 | once | SFP_START, SFP_POLL | handle_sfp_slot(i); ends on a board without slot i | 50 ms, task_until(last + 10) |
| 10 | house | once | HOUSE_START, HOUSE_SEC | house_second(): loader confirm, uptime register, button, link_irq, ARP timer | 1 s, task_until(last + 200) |

Where D2 keeps today's behaviour rather than modules.md's later shape (for
old-vs-new): stp, lacp, lldp run each tick into their own timers (not 20 ms
/ 1 s wakes); the link check's 1 s fallback is house's link_irq, as today;
rx has no 1 s fallback (today has none); loop doesn't wake on EV_LINKCHG
(nothing posts it yet); no igmp task (IGMP has no timers: rx-driven); the
cable test's tick rides in lldp's step until it becomes a job (E); sfp is
today's per-slot pin poll (the #770 states are E2). The `reset` command
still calls reset_chip() (no record); ^R ^R and the watchdog record. HEALTH
builds' per-phase counters (health_phase) are no longer fed: the scheduler's
per-state numbers (`health tasks`) replace them.

## D2 fix: long console output kicks the watchdog
con_putc() kicks the watchdog when a wait for TX room ends (watchdog.md).
schedtest base image, `peek 0 49000` (98 KB of hex in one CLI_RUN step) in rtlsim:
  with the kick:    49000 of 49000 bytes printed in 9.0 s; no reset
  without (1f900eb): 27888 bytes, then "Last reset: watchdog in cli/CLI_RUN after 5000 ms"
A non-printing hang still resets: sim_neg hang, the watchdog at the 1000th and
999th tick. make schedtest-sim --twice: 0 failures, deterministic (64 s);
host quick 20 PASS; 8x gate PASS (IRAM 0, common 0x3e5d, 291 B left).
rtlsim's control mode doesn't learn MACs (learning lives in its OVS fabric),
so an l2 table of ~2000 entries couldn't be built here; peek is the long print.
