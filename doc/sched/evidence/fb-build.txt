## Against the evidence

- Common area: 163 B left here; E4's f336874 gave 169 B
  (e4-polls-vlan-eee.txt). The commits after it (3e204d6's swdt_on test in
  the resident hw_timeout(), 3b7886c) are the likely 6 B; not checked one by
  one.
- xdata: XSEG ends 0x50f4 (XISEG to 0x5109) here; e4-polls-vlan-eee.txt
  says "XSEG ends at 0x5ef2 with the bitmap". That figure is from the
  byte-map experiment (vlan_on[4096]) that E4 tried and dropped for the
  bitmap (the orchestrator, 2026-11-20); the bitmap build ends at 0x50f4.
